Everything SmartDoc does.

A tour of the app, one feature at a time. Scroll to see each one at work, in the app itself.

Review and draft

Suggestions in the margin

Each suggestion sits beside the text it’s about, marked by kind: wording, content, questions, structure and design. Click one to open it in full, in a card you can move anywhere.

Chat

Your own AI, beside your document

Work with Claude or ChatGPT in SmartDesktop’s chat, signed in with your own account. It’s a window of its own: move it out of the way whenever you like.

Review and draft

Choices, not just changes

Where there’s more than one good answer, your AI offers options. Pick one, or none of them, and confirm.

Review and draft

Edit any suggestion

Accept a change as it is, or reword it first. Add a comment and your AI takes it into account.

Review and draft

Talk it over

The chat knows which suggestion you’re looking at. Say what you think, and your AI can rework it for you to decide on.

Review and draft

Questions only you can answer

Your AI asks for the facts it doesn’t have, like a result or a number, and uses your answer.

Review and draft

Structure and design

Moves, layout and design changes sit in the left margin, decided the same way.

Review and draft

Comments of your own

Select any text and say what you want. Your AI responds in its review or carries it out in the next draft. It works on a draft preview too, so you can ask for a fix before you accept.

Review and draft

The chat follows along

What you open and decide is noted in the chat, so your AI knows what you mean by “this”. The chat stays one conversation, with the decisions where they belong: beside the page.

Drafts

Every draft kept

Pick any draft from the list in the top bar. Each keeps its reviews, and the chat notes which one you opened. An older draft stays as it was: to work on one, your AI makes a variation or a new document from it.

Drafts

See what changed

Put the draft preview beside the draft it came from, with removed and added text highlighted. Step through the changes one at a time before you accept.

Focuses

A focus for each review

Each review has a focus: what it’s for. Ask for a new one in the chat, or write it here and copy a prompt for your own AI agent.

Review and highlight

The key information

Highlights for your purpose, grouped by category and ranked by importance.

Review and highlight

What’s missing

Your AI flags what you’d expect to find but the document doesn’t say.

Review and highlight

Read it as a list

Switch to List for every highlight in one place, the most important first.

Setup

No Terminal needed

SmartDesktop installs Claude Code or Codex with its maker’s own installer, showing how far it’s got, then opens the sign-in page. It never sees your sign-in.

Your own AI agent

Bring your own AI agent

Claude Desktop, Claude Code and other AI agents can work in SmartDoc too, without SmartDesktop, each in a window of its own. Several can work at once, even on the same document, and can’t overwrite each other.

Home

Documents, variations, drafts

The home screen lists your documents, each one’s variations and every draft, with where each review stands.

Variations

A version for each purpose

A variation for each job or audience, with its own drafts, sharing the ones it started from.

Documents

A new document from any draft

Turn an improved draft into a document of its own, ready for its own variations.

Try it on your own documents.

Free for macOS, with SmartDesktop or your own AI agent. In beta, and still changing significantly.
