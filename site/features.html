Everything SmartDoc does.

A tour of the app, one feature at a time. Scroll to see each one at work, in the app itself.

Review and draft

Suggestions in the margin

Each suggestion sits beside the text it’s about, coloured by kind: wording, content, questions, structure and design.

Review and draft

Choices, not just changes

Where there’s more than one good answer, your agent offers options. Pick one, or none of them.

Review and draft

Edit any suggestion

Accept a change as it is, or reword it first. Add a comment and your agent takes it into account.

Review and draft

Questions only you can answer

Your agent asks for the facts it doesn’t have, like a result or a number, and uses your answer.

Review and draft

Structure and design

Moves, layout and design changes sit in the left margin, decided the same way.

Review and draft

See what’s left

The bar shows how far you’ve got. Switch to To do to hide what you’ve decided.

Drafts

Every draft kept

Step back to any draft with the arrows in the top bar, and on again. Each one keeps its reviews.

Focuses

A focus for each review

Each review has a focus: what it’s for. Start a new one by saying what you’re after.

Review and highlight

The key information

Highlights for your purpose, grouped by category and ranked by importance.

Review and highlight

What’s missing

Your agent flags what you’d expect to find but the document doesn’t say.

Review and highlight

Read it as a list

Switch to List for every highlight in one place, the most important first.

Home

Documents, variations, drafts

The home screen lists your documents, each one’s variations and every draft, with where each review stands.

Variations

A version for each purpose

A variation for each job or audience, with its own drafts, sharing the ones it started from.

Documents

A new document from any draft

Turn an improved draft into a document of its own, ready for its own variations.
