New in SmartDoc 0.19 Experimental

Open Word, Excel and PowerPoint files.

SmartDoc turns them into PDFs itself, without Office or any other app, so you can review them with your AI like any other document. The original is kept with it.

Turn it on

Off until you want it.

It’s experimental, so it’s off to start with. Once it’s on, files open the way PDFs do.

  1. 1

    Open Settings

    In SmartDoc, choose SmartDoc › Settings… (⌘,), and under Experimental turn on Open Word, Excel and PowerPoint files.

  2. 2

    Open a file

    Drop it on SmartDoc, choose File › Open…, or ask your AI to open it. SmartDoc turns it into a PDF in a moment, and opens it.

  3. 3

    Review it

    Suggestions, highlights, drafts and variations all work as they do for a PDF. If anything didn’t come out as it is, SmartDoc says so as it opens, and tells your AI too.

What it opens

Laid out as Office lays them out.

Each kind of file has a layout engine of its own, written to do what Office does: where lines break, where pages end, how a sheet prints.

  • .docx

    Word

    Styles, lists and numbering, tables, headers and footers with page numbers, footnotes, sections and columns, tabs and leaders, pictures.

  • .xlsx

    Excel

    Every sheet as it prints: its print area and titles, paper, margins and scale, number formats, fonts, fills and borders, merged cells, headers and footers.

  • .pptx

    PowerPoint

    A page a slide, with its layout and theme: text, bullets and numbering, shapes, pictures and tables. Hidden slides are left out, as PowerPoint leaves them out.

  • .md .txt .png .jpg

    And more

    Markdown, plain text and pictures open too. Older .doc, .xls and .ppt files don’t: save them in the newer format first.

Measured

Held to Office’s own PDFs.

Every test document is turned into a PDF by SmartDoc, by Office and by LibreOffice. SmartDoc’s has to be at least as close to Office’s as LibreOffice’s is: the same pages, each line broken in the same place and set where Office sets it, and the same ink on the page.

Measured on SmartDoc’s own test documents against Word, Excel and PowerPoint 16 for Mac. Excel follows Excel for Mac, which prints a sheet a little larger than Excel for Windows. Where Office’s fonts aren’t on your Mac, SmartDoc uses stand-ins with the same measurements (for Calibri, Cambria, Arial, Times New Roman and Courier New), so lines still break in the same places.

Not yet

What’s still to come.

SmartDoc tells you when a document has any of these, so nothing goes missing without you knowing.

  • Charts in Excel and PowerPoint aren’t drawn yet.
  • Gradients show as a single colour.
  • Text boxes and shapes in Word documents aren’t drawn yet, and floating pictures show in line with the text.
  • Conditional formatting in Excel isn’t shown: cells keep their own formatting.
  • Rotated text in Excel cells shows level.
  • Fonts with no stand-in, such as Calibri Light without Office installed, are shown in the nearest font, so some lines may break differently.
