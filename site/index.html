Two tools

Improve it, or understand it.

Each kind of review has its own colour, so it’s always clear what you’re looking at.

Review and draft

Your AI suggests changes for you to accept or reject, then writes the next draft from your decisions.

  • Changes, choices of wording, questions and design notes, each beside the text it’s about
  • Edit a suggestion, comment on it, or add changes of your own
  • Every draft is kept, so you can step back to any of them

Review and highlight

Your AI picks out the key information for what you’re looking for, including anything the document doesn’t say.

  • Highlights on the page, grouped and ranked by importance
  • A focus for each question you ask, kept side by side
  • Read them on the document, or as a list

How it works

You stay in charge of every change.

SmartDoc is the desk; your AI does the reading. Nothing changes until you’ve decided.

Ask your AI

Tell Claude what you want, such as “get my CV to two pages”. SmartDoc opens with your document.

Decide in the app

Step through each suggestion in the margin. Accept, reject, edit or answer it, at your own pace.

Get the next draft

Your AI writes the next draft from your decisions. Compare drafts, go round again, or save it as a PDF.

Variations

One document, a version for every purpose.

Improve your CV once, then make a variation for each job, or a version of a proposal for each audience. Each variation has its own drafts and starts from whichever draft you choose, so the others stay as they are.

Install

Add it to your AI in a minute.

SmartDoc connects to your AI app. It opens by itself whenever your AI needs it.

Claude Desktop

Install it as an extension.

  1. Download for Claude Desktop
  2. Open the downloaded file. Claude Desktop shows what it installs; choose Install.
  3. Start a new chat and ask Claude to review your document.

Claude Code

Add it with one command.

  1. Run this in a terminal: 
    claude mcp add --scope user smartdoc -- npx -y @smartdoc/mcp
  2. Start a new Claude Code session and ask Claude to review your document.

Needs Node.js 18 or later. Other AI apps that run MCP servers can use npx -y @smartdoc/mcp.

Good to know

Private by design.

Stays on your computer

Your document and its drafts live on your computer. Your AI only sees what it reads through SmartDoc, and the app only accepts connections from your own computer.

Free, no account

There’s nothing to sign up for. The AI is your own, under your own subscription.

Checked downloads

The first time it starts, it downloads the app from the project’s GitHub releases and checks it against the release’s checksums. Runs on macOS (Apple silicon or Intel); Windows is planned.
