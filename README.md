# SmartDoc

Your documents, reviewed by your own agent.

SmartDoc is a Mac app that shows your document while an AI agent you already use, such as Claude Desktop or Claude Code, works through it with you. The agent can suggest changes for you to accept or reject, then write the next draft from your decisions. Or it can pick out the information that matters for what you're looking for. Either way, you decide what happens to your document.

- **Website:** [smartdoc.tw050x.net](https://smartdoc.tw050x.net), with a demo and the download
- **npm:** [@smartdoc/cli](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@smartdoc/cli), the command that installs SmartDoc and connects your agents
